CSV import requirements

Minimum useful columns: Player, Team, Pos, Rank, GP, MIN, FG%, FT%, 3PM, PTS, REB, AST, STL, BLK.

For accurate volume-weighted FG% and FT% valuation, include FGM, FGA, FTM, FTA when available. If those fields are missing, the app estimates shooting attempts from public 2025–26 NBA rates per minute, scales them to projected minutes, and uses your imported FG%/FT% to estimate makes. These estimates are labeled and are less reliable for rookies or players with changed roles.

Shooting-volume estimates will load automatically when public historical stats are reachable.

Optional: Risk (Low/Medium/High), Sleeper (yes/no), Notes. Paste/import only projections you have permission to use.
